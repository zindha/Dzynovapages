Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-04

NetFusion is a download manager built by Dzynova Technologies. This policy describes what information the app handles.

The short version

NetFusion collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It never sends your information anywhere except to the download URL you explicitly ask it to fetch.

What the app stores on your device

  • Download list (URLs, file names, sizes, progress) in the app's private storage so downloads can pause and resume.
  • Downloaded files, saved to your device's public Downloads/NetFusion folder via the Android media store.
  • Usage counters (bytes moved over Wi-Fi vs cellular) used for the in-app dashboard and daily cellular quota. These stay on the device.

Network activity

The app connects only to the download URLs you enter or share into it, solely to download the file you requested. It does not communicate with us or any third-party service.

Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to download your files.
  • CHANGE_NETWORK_STATE — to request the Wi-Fi and cellular networks used for bonded downloads.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC — to keep downloads running with a visible notification.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to show download progress (Android 13+).
  • WAKE_LOCK — to keep downloads alive while the screen is off.

Children

NetFusion is a general-purpose download tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page with any change to the app's data handling.
