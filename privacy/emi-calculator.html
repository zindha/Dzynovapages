2. Data We Handle

On-device only. Loan amounts, interest rates, tenure, EMI results, comparison sessions, prepayment plans, saved history, favorites, and reminder schedules are stored locally on your device using on-device storage (Hive). They are not uploaded to our servers — we do not operate a backend for this app.

We do not collect personal identifiers. We do not ask for your name, phone number, address, or government IDs, and we do not collect precise location, contacts, or files outside the app's own storage. Exports (PDF/CSV/XLSX/JSON backup) are created only when you tap Export/Share, and you choose where to save or share them.

Backups: History + reminders can be exported as a JSON backup file via Settings → Backup & Restore. A daily auto-snapshot (when enabled) is written to the app's private documents folder on the device and is not uploaded to any cloud by default. If you enable cloud sync (future Drive app-data folder integration), that backup would be stored in your own Google Drive app-data folder under your Google account.

3. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs. All runtime permissions are requested in context and can be denied:

  • INTERNET — to load ads (when not on the ad-free premium), to check Play Billing product/entitlement, and to open the privacy policy and store listing in your browser.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) — only if you create an EMI reminder. You are prompted at the moment you enable a reminder; denying it only disables reminders.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — to query and complete the one-time Remove Ads in-app purchase via Google Play Billing.

The app does not request access to your files, camera, microphone, contacts, location, or phone state.

4. Third-Party Services

The app integrates only the following third-party SDKs. Each service applies its own privacy policy:

Advertising — Google AdMob

  • Displays banner ads on the free tier. AdMob may use the Advertising ID, IP address, and coarse device signals to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have consented and where AdMob is configured to do so. See AdMob & AdSense Publisher Data and How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.
  • You can reset or delete the Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. In the EEA/UK/Switzerland you are shown a consent dialog (via Google's UMP) before personalized ads are served; see §5.
  • Until you publish production AdMob IDs, builds use Google's official test ad units — these never bill and carry no revenue.

In-App Purchases — Google Play Billing

  • The Remove Ads one-time purchase is fulfilled by Google Play Billing and tied to your Google account. Billing status is checked via Play Services; payment is handled entirely by Google. See Google Play Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

Platform services

As an Android app distributed via Google Play, the app may interact with Google Play Services for billing, ad serving, and package visibility. We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, Facebook/Meta SDKs, or behavioral remarketing SDKs. If that changes, this policy and the Play Console Data safety section will be updated first.

5. Ads, Consent & Your Controls

Premium removes ads entirely. After the one-time purchase the app no longer requests ads.

  • EEA/UK/Switzerland: on first launch (and whenever required) the app shows Google's consent dialog (UMP) to collect your choices for personalized vs. non-personalized ads. You can change your choice anytime from Settings → Privacy Policy (consent reset where available) or via Google's Ads Settings and Android's Privacy → Ads settings.
  • Opting out of personalized ads does not remove ads — it shows non-personalized ads based on context rather than your Advertising ID / browsing history.
  • Notifications & reminders: disable at any time from system Settings → Apps → EMI Calculator → Notifications, or delete the reminder inside the app.
  • Clear local data: use Android Settings → Apps → EMI Calculator → Storage → Clear data, or inside the app delete history entries and reminders individually.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the third-party services listed in §4 to the extent necessary to provide their features (e.g., AdMob receives ad requests; Google Play Billing receives purchase/entitlement checks). We do not share loan inputs with ad networks.

7. Data Retention & Security

  • Local history, comparison sessions, prepayment plans, and reminders remain on device until you delete them or clear app storage.
  • Ad requests and billing entitlements are handled by Google under Google's retention policies.
  • We apply reasonable safeguards (on-device storage only, no server copy, TLS for network calls). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but minimizing collection is our primary control.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is a general-purpose financial calculator not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child's data has been provided, contact us at zindhak@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. International Users — GDPR & CCPA

EEA / UK (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or port data relating to you, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent (e.g., personalized ads). To exercise these rights, contact us at zindhak@gmail.com and, for ad personalization, use the in-app consent dialog and Android Privacy → Ads controls.

California (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale/share of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA. Ad personalization can be limited via the controls in §5. To make a request, contact us at zindhak@gmail.com; we verify requests and respond within the timelines required by law.

We do not discriminate against users who exercise privacy rights.
